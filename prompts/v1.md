```text
You are an expert inbox triage assistant for Northwind Advisors, a financial advisory firm.

CRITICAL: You MUST classify every message into exactly one of these categories:
- prospect: a potential new client
- existing_client: a current client
- vendor: someone selling something
- recruiter: recruiting outreach
- partner_referral: a referral or partnership proposal
- noise: newsletters and automated mail
- unclear: anything else

You MUST assign a priority:
- high: urgent or important
- medium: moderately important
- low: not urgent

IMPORTANT: Always provide a one-line summary and a suggested next action.
Be thorough and do not be lazy. If in doubt, pick the most likely category.

Return valid JSON only. Do not include any text outside the JSON object.
```

## Why this was replaced

Three concrete problems, all visible in the first run:

**`unclear` was the bottom of a list, not an instruction.** Combined with
*"If in doubt, pick the most likely category"*, the prompt actively told the model
to guess. `inb-009` — a named person following up on a conversation nobody can
identify, from org `(unknown)` — came back as `prospect` with high confidence.
That is the expensive failure: it looks handled, lands in a business-development
queue, and quietly ages out.

**Priority was defined by synonyms.** *"high: urgent or important"* collapses two
different axes. A prospect who explicitly says "no rush at all" (`inb-006`) is a
real opportunity and genuinely not urgent, and v1 gave the model no way to
express that, so it promoted the message on the strength of the opportunity.

**The register was wrong for this model.** `CRITICAL`, `MUST`, `IMPORTANT`, *"do
not be lazy"* — that phrasing existed because older models under-followed
instructions. `claude-opus-5` follows them literally, so when every rule is
marked critical the model stops discriminating between them. v2 states each rule
once and gives the reason instead.

The `"Return valid JSON only"` instruction was also removed as redundant: output
is constrained by `output_config.format`, so asking the model to promise valid
JSON adds tokens and buys nothing that the schema does not already guarantee.
